About SriPedia

Exploring Sri Lanka's rich heritage through technology

Our Mission

SriPedia aims to make Sri Lankan history, culture, and knowledge accessible to everyone through innovative technology and interactive learning experiences.

We believe in preserving cultural heritage while making it engaging for modern learners through AI-powered tools, collaborative classrooms, and interactive quizzes.
