Getting Started with SriPedia

Welcome to SriPedia, your comprehensive learning platform for Sri Lankan history and culture!

What is SriPedia?

SriPedia is an educational platform that uses AI-powered tools to help you learn about Sri Lanka's rich history, culture, and heritage.

How to Navigate SriPedia

Use the navigation bar at the top of the page to access different features:

  • Home: Your dashboard with recent activity
  • Chatbot: Ask questions about Sri Lanka
  • Classrooms: Join virtual classrooms
  • Quizzes: Test your knowledge
  • Files: Upload and manage your documents
